# Ashenreach — X Launch Thread

## Thread (post 1 of 10)
We built a complete open-world action RPG that runs in your browser. No install. Free. It is called Ashenreach. Thread on what it is and why we made it.

## Thread (post 2 of 10)
The pitch: the highland is on fire and the dead will not rest. You are the Warden, carrying souls across a burning land to the cairns that hold them. Every soul you carry is a debt.

## Thread (post 3 of 10)
Core loop: explore, fight, pick up a soul, reach a cairn, bank it for score. Or push on and risk the Embertide, a difficulty tide that rises the longer you linger.

## Thread (post 4 of 10)
Combat has weight. Swings, parries, dashes. Four weapons with different reach and timing: blade, maul, spear, censer. Break a guard, build a combo, end the run on the Colossus.

## Thread (post 5 of 10)
We measured it. Headless boot test: 374 draw calls, 43k triangles, 10 enemies spawned, zero console errors, mean luminance 0.32 (not a black screen). Evidence over vibes.

## Thread (post 6 of 10)
Every asset is made in-repo. Procedural GLB models, PBR texture sets, UI icons. Zero third-party art licences. 22 models, 10 texture sets, 16 icons, all deterministic from a seed.

## Thread (post 7 of 10)
Progression sticks. Equipment, loadouts, and a leaderboard save in your browser. The run is short. The studio is forever.

## Thread (post 8 of 10)
It is free. WebGL2, desktop and mobile. Open the link and play. No store page, no download, no wait.

## Thread (post 9 of 10)
What is next: daily seeds, more biomes, and a harder Colossus phase. We ship in the open and tune from real runs.
