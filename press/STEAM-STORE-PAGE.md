## About the Game

The highland is on fire, and the dead will not rest. In **Ashenreach** you play the Warden — a lone bearer tasked with carrying the souls of the fallen across a hostile, procedurally-stitched landscape to the cairns that will hold them.

Every soul you bear is a debt. The longer you carry them, the heavier the dark grows. Bank them at a cairn for score and safety, or risk the **Embertide** — a rising tide of difficulty — for one more before the world swallows the run.

Combat has weight: timing-based swings, parries, and dashes against husks, wights, ash-hounds, and the **Colossus** that wakes when you've carried too much. Four weapon archetypes (blade, maul, spear, censer) change reach, swing-time, and stagger. Every drop rolls affixes. The run is short. The studio is forever.

## Features

- **Carry, don't hoard.** A transparent score model that rewards banking souls over hoarding them — risk vs. reward in every step.
- **The Embertide.** A difficulty system that escalates pressure the longer you linger.
- **Four weapons, distinct feel.** Blade, maul, spear, censer — each a different rhythm of combat.
- **Procedural highland.** Peat lowlands, moss slopes, slate ruins, mire hollows, and the embertide crest — a new stretch of land every run.
- **Boss: the Colossus.** A multi-phase fight that ends the run on your terms.
- **Progression that sticks.** Equipment, loadouts, and a leaderboard persist in your browser.
- **Zero install.** WebGL2, desktop and mobile. Free to play.
