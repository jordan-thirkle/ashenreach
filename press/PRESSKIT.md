## Description (short)
Carry the dead home. Ashenreach is a browser-native open-world action RPG: cross a burning highland, bear the souls of the fallen to the cairns, and outrun the rising Embertide.

## Description (long)
The highland burns and the dead will not rest. You are the Warden, a lone bearer carrying souls across a hostile, procedurally-stitched land to the cairns that hold them. Every soul is a debt; the longer you carry, the heavier the dark. Bank them for score or risk the Embertide for one more. Combat has weight — timing, parries, dashes — against husks, wights, ash-hounds, and the Colossus. Free, no install, WebGL2.

## History
Ashenreach is built by By JTT, a small studio shipping AI-native browser games. The game is generated end-to-end: code, procedural models, PBR textures, and UI icons are produced in-repo with zero third-party art licences.

## Features
- Carry-don't-hoard score model
- The Embertide escalating difficulty
- Four weapons with distinct feel
- Procedural highland, five biomes
- Boss: the Colossus
- Persistent progression + leaderboard
- Zero install, free to play
