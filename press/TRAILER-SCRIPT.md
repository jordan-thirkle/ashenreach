# Ashenreach - Trailer Script

Runtime target: 90 seconds (a 30-second cut is noted where it diverges).
Voice-over: plain, specific, evidence-led. No hype words, no em dashes, no emoji.
On-screen text uses the same plain register. All visuals are generated in-engine; no external art.

## Tone and pacing

Slow open. Ash, wind, a single figure. Build to the Embertide and the Crown. Hold the tagline for two full seconds at the end. Music is low and percussive, generated in code, no licensed audio.

---

## Shot list

**00:00 - 00:08 | Cold open**
- Visual: Static wide of the Ashflats. Ash falls like snow. Wind pushes the flakes sideways. No UI.
- VO: "An old war burned the sky. Ash falls like snow."
- 30s cut: this shot holds to 00:04 then hard cuts to the tagline.

**00:08 - 00:16 | The Warden**
- Visual: A Cairnwarden walks into frame, stoops, and lifts a faint glowing soul from the ground. The soul trails behind as a soft light.
- VO: "You are a Cairnwarden. Your work is to carry the dead home."
- On-screen: "Carry the dead home."

**00:16 - 00:26 | The risk dial**
- Visual: The Warden carries two souls. Movement is visibly slower. A sound ring pulses wider around them. A hound turns its head toward the ring.
- VO: "Carrying slows you. It makes you louder. That is the risk."
- On-screen: "Slower. Louder."

**00:26 - 00:36 | The fight**
- Visual: Quick cuts. Husk shatters under a weapon swing. A wight rises from Wetmoor fog. A fallen warden on a Grey Crags ridge looses an arrow that bends around a pine.
- VO: "Nine enemies stand in the way. Husks. Hounds. Wights. Fallen wardens."
- 30s cut: insert this block after the tagline.

**00:36 - 00:46 | The cairn**
- Visual: The Warden reaches a cairn stone. The carried souls settle into it. A calm pulse of light. XP and ember numbers tick up on a minimal HUD.
- VO: "Deliver a soul and you bank XP and embers."
- On-screen: "+XP  +Embers"

**00:46 - 00:56 | The branches**
- Visual: Three skill icons resolve: Warden (a shield shape), Ember (a flame shape), Wake (a soul shape). Each shows a rank pip. 15 skills total, quick count-up.
- VO: "Spend embers on Warden, Ember, or Wake. Fifteen skills."
- On-screen: "3 branches. 15 skills."

**00:56 - 01:08 | The Embertide**
- Visual: A timer bar climbs. The sky reddens. Ash thickens to a storm. Loot glints brighter on the ground. The Warden pushes forward through the Scorch.
- VO: "The Embertide rises. The world gets harder. The loot gets richer."
- On-screen: "Embertide rising"

**01:08 - 01:20 | The Crown**
- Visual: The central crater. The Ashen Crown, a colossus of stacked cairn stone, looms. The Warden stands small at its base. Cut to black on the swing.
- VO: "Every run ends at the Ashen Crown."
- 30s cut: ends on the Crown at 00:30, then tagline.

**01:20 - 01:30 | Close**
- Visual: Logo on ash-grey. Play button forms from falling ash.
- VO: "Ashenreach. Free, in your browser. Carry the dead home."
- On-screen: "Ashenreach. Free to play. ashenreach.example"
- Hold tagline two seconds.

## Asset notes

- All shots rendered in-engine from the live build. Capture at 1920x1080, 60fps.
- No licensed music or sound. Audio is synthesized in code.
- Use the actual Ashflats, Wetmoor, Grey Crags, and Scorch builds for those shots. Do not fake biomes.
- Keep HUD minimal on screen. Numbers are real in-game values.
- VO recorded dry, no reverb, plain delivery. One take per line.
