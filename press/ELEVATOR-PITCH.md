# Elevator Pitch

Ashenreach is a free, install-free action RPG that runs in your browser. You play the Warden, carrying the souls of the dead across a burning highland to the cairns that will hold them — while the Embertide, a tide of the world's grief, rises the longer you linger.

It is a short, replayable loop about weight and choice: bank a soul for safety and score, or carry it into the dark for more. Four weapons with distinct feel, a multi-phase boss, and a procedurally stitched world that is different every run.

What makes it different: the entire game — code, models, textures, icons, and audio — is generated in-repo with zero third-party licences, and it ships with its own automated QA report. We prove it runs; we do not ask you to trust that it does.
