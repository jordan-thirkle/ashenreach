# 01 — Overview

## Vision
Ashenreach is a browser-native action RPG that respects the player's time. A run is 10–20 minutes: cross a stretch of burning highland, carry the souls of the fallen to the cairns, and get out before the Embertide takes you. No installs, no accounts, no grind walls.

## Audience
- Core: action-RPG and soulslike players who want a tight, skill-based loop.
- Casual: players who will open a link and play for one commute.
- Browser-native audience: people who will not download a client.

## Design pillars

1. **Weight over power.** Progression is about what you carry and risk, not stat inflation.
2. **Evidence over hype.** Every system is measurable; the build ships with its own QA report.
3. **Restartable.** Death is a full reset of the run but keeps studio progression.
4. **No licences.** All art and audio are generated in-repo; the game is fully ownable.

## Scope (v1)
- Single biome-stitched open world, 5 biome types.
- 4 weapon archetypes, 3 enemy tiers + 1 boss.
- Cairn banking, Embertide, daily seeds, local leaderboard.
- Desktop + mobile (touch).

## Success metrics
- Boot success rate > 99% on WebGL2 browsers.
- Median run length 8–14 minutes.
- Banking-vs-hoarding split shows real risk taking (not all-safe play).
