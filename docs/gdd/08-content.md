## Weapons (archetypes)
- Blade: fast, low stagger, 2.4m reach.
- Maul: slow, high stagger, 1.8m reach.
- Spear: longest reach, pokes.
- Censer: ember DoT aura, medium.

## Items
- Consumables: draught (heal), ward (temp resist), ember-phial (bank boost).
- Materials: soul-shard (upgrade), cairn-dust (craft).
- Relics: passive modifiers (e.g. +soul XP).
