# 09 — Monetization

## Model: free-to-play, no paywall
Ashenreach is free in the browser. There is no gameplay gating behind payment.

## Allowed (v1)
- **Optional supporter tip** (one-time, no in-game benefit). Clearly labeled, off by default.
- **Cosmetic cloak tints** (palette-locked, no stat change). Future.
- **No loot boxes, no energy, no ads.**

## Prohibited
- Pay-to-win stats, weapon unlocks, or souls.
- Time gates / wait-to-play.
- Third-party ad networks (privacy + tone).

## Sustainability (studio)
- By JTT funds development from the broader catalog; Ashenreach is a flagship demo of AI-native browser shipping.
- Future: supporter tier on the site, branded cairn dedications (cosmetic, opt-in).
