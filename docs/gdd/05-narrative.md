# 05 — Narrative

## The Warden
You are the Warden: a bearer with no name in the record, sworn to carry the dead to the cairns. The role is inherited, not chosen. The cloak you wear has held a thousand souls.

## The Dead
When a person dies on the highland, their soul clings to the living rather than passing. Left un-carried, a soul curdles into a husk — a walking grudge. Every enemy you fight was once a person who was not carried in time.

## The Cairns
Stone rings built by earlier Wardens. A soul banked at a cairn is at rest; it cannot be taken by the Embertide. Lighting a new cairn extends the safe network and is the only lasting mark a Warden leaves.

## The Embertide
A tide of ash and old grief that rises as the highland forgets the living. It is not a villain; it is the world's grief, and it grows when the dead are left to wait. Banking souls quiets it.

## Tone
Restrained, elegiac, folk-mythic. No heroes, no villains — only weight, and the choice to keep carrying.
